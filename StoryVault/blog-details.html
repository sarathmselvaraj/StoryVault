Preserving Rare Archives: A Guide for Collectors

Key techniques, environmental controls, and handling practices for long-term manuscript and rare book preservation.

David Sterling Jul 20, 2026 7 min read

Archival preservation balances access with conservation, extending the life of historic parchment, paper, and leather bindings for future generations.

Control relative humidity and lighting

Maintain consistent temperatures and keep relative humidity around 45–50%. Direct ultraviolet light degrades cellulose and fades iron gall ink rapidly over time.

Handle manuscripts with clean, dry hands

Contrary to old myths, clean dry hands provide better tactile control than cotton gloves, preventing accidental tears while avoiding harmful skin oils.

Store in acid-free enclosures

Protect delicate volumes in archival-grade clamshell boxes lined with acid-free tissue paper to buffer against atmospheric pollutants.
