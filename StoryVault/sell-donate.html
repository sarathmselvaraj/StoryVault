Sell or Donate Books

Trade your gently used books for store credit or donate to local school reading programs.

Guidelines & Process

1. Accepted Conditions

We accept Hardcover and Paperback books in Mint, Excellent, or Good condition. No missing pages, water damage, or heavy highlighting.

2. Valuation & Store Credit

Books sold to StoryVault receive up to 40% of original cover price in direct cash or 60% in StoryVault library store credit.

3. Donation Impact

Donated books are sanitized, cataloged, and delivered directly to rural community libraries and youth reading clubs.
