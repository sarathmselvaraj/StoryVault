Isolated in the red rust dunes of the Martian Elysium Planitia, astrobiologist Dr. Evelyn Zhao tends to humanity's most ambitious experiment: a towering geodesic greenhouse sheltering Earth's rarest botanical specimens. But when deep subterranean roots penetrate an ancient geothermal fissure, a dormant alien bioluminescent flora awakens, broadcasting complex luminous pulses across the Martian night sky.
